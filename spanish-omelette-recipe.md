# Recipe for Spanish omelette


## Ingredients

- **8** potatoes
- **4** eggs
- **1** white onion
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

- cut the potatoes in thin slices
- fry the potatoes in plenty of olive oil until soft and golden
- cut the onion in small pieces
- fry the onion until golden
- add eggs to a bowl and whisk until mixed
- add the fried potatoes and the fried onions to the eggs and combine
- add salt and pepper to taste
- add a thin layer of olive oil to a low pan
- add the mixture of eggs, potatoes, and onion to the pan and let it fry for 7-10 min, shaking the pan on and off to make sure the omelette doesn't stick to the pan
- flip the omelette (you can use a big plate for this, or a spanish "omelette flip" plate if you have one), and fry on the other side
- serve warm and enjoy!
