# Potatoe pancakes


## Ingredients

- **2-3** raw eggs.
- **5-8** potatoes.



## Instructions

1. **Preheat pan:**
   - Preheat your pan.

2. **Grade potatoes:**
   - Grate potatoes with medium-thin grade.

3. **Add eggs:**
   - Stir grated potatoes and eggs in a bowl until the mixture is uniform.
4. **Fry the pancakes**
   - Take a handful of the mixture, squeeze out extra liquid and put on a pan.
