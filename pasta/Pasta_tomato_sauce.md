# Delicious Pasta With Tomato Sauce!

## Ingredients for 4 servings

- Spaghetti 320g
- Peeled tomatoes 800g
- Extra virgin olive oil 30g
- Garlic 1 clove
- Basil to taste
- Fine salt to taste


## Instructions

- Heat olive oil + garlic for 2 min.
- Add peeled tomatoes + salt.
- Cover and simmer on low for 1 hour.
- Remove garlic; blend/strain tomatoes.
- Add sauce + basil; simmer briefly.
- Cook spaghetti in salted water.
- Drain al dente into sauce.
- Toss on high heat.
- Serve with fresh basil.
