# NAWWABI VERMECELLI

## Ingredients

- Vermecelli, I Packet
- Brown SUGAR, HALF CUP
- Clarified butter
- Cardamom, 7
- Almonds, 10-15
- Milk,  1 litre
- Custard Powder, 3-4 tbsp
- Condensed Milk, 3 tbsp


## Instructions

- Put clarified butter in wok and stir fry cardamom.
- Add vermecelli and fry for 2minutes. After its aromatic add sugar and fry it for 2 more minutes.
- Add almonds and keep it aside.
- Take another pan and add milk.
- Boil milk and add custard powder. Cook until thickens
- Now put half vermecelli in serving dish, then add custrad mixture
- Then put second layer of vermecelli.
- Put in refrigerator and serve chilled
