## Ingredients:
### Crust
- 250g crushed biscuits
- 1/2 cup melted butter

### Filling
- 225g cream cheese
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbs corn flour
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp lemon zest
- 1 cup condensed milk

### Topping
- 380g frozen blueberries
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbs lemon juice
- 2 tsp corn flour
- 3 tbs water
