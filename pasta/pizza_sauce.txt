Lily's pizza sauce recipe

Ingredients
A lot of olive oil
Two large onions
One small garlic
Dried oregano, thyme and marjoram
800g tomato pasata
dash sugar
salt and pepper

Instructions
Fry onions and garlic until transparent, add oregano, thyme and marjoram in olive oil until sizzling, (do not burn)
Add tomato paste and a dash of sugar
Cook down with a lid for 30 minutes
Season with salt and pepper
