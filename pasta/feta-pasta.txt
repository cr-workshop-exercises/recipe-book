# Feta pasta

## Ingredients
- Pasta
- Cherry tomatoes
- Basil
- Garlic
- Olive poil

## Instructions
- Cut tomatoes in half
- Chop garlic
- Add cut tomatoes, garlic, a block of feta and olive oil into a baking tray
- Bake at 200 degrees celsius in an oven
- Cook pasta
- Take oven tray out
- Mix cooked pasta and the mixture from oven
- Add basil
