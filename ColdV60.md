# Recipe name

## Ingredients

- coffee
- water
- ice


## Instructions

- boils the water to 88C
- wet your filter
- put the coffee on the filter
- put ice 150 grams
- turn on the balance machine in mil
- make sure it is at Zero
- put 50 mil of the hot water and wait for 30 seconds
- after 30 seconds, add another 50 mil of water and wait for 30 seconds
- repeat it untile you reach 150 mil
- then, enjoy it!
