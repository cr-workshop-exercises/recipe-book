# Lemon Chia seed Pudding

## Ingredients (2–3 servings):  
- 1 ¼ cups milk of choice (almond, oat, or dairy) 
- ¼ cup chia seeds 
- Zest of 1 lemon 
- 2–3 tbsp fresh lemon juice 
- 1–2 tbsp maple syrup or honey (to taste) 
- Optional: vanilla extract or extra lemon zest for topping

## Instructions:
- In a bowl or jar, whisk together milk, lemon juice, lemon zest, and sweetener. 
- Stir in chia seeds until evenly combined. 
- Let sit for 10 minutes, stir again to prevent clumping. 
- Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight until thick and creamy. 
- Stir before serving and add extra lemon zest if desired.
