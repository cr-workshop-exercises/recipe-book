Chickpea curry recipe

Ingredients
2 tbsp neutral oil
1 large onion
1 large carrot
1 bell pepper
4 gloves garlic
1 chilli (to taste)
thumb sized piece of fresh ginger
2 cans chickpeas
1 tbsp tomato puree
1 can coconut milk
200ml vegetable stock
1/2 lime
Fresh cilantro
Indian spice mix


Instructions
Heat  oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the veg, sautéing until softened.
Add the garlic, ginger and chilli and cook for 1 minute
All the tomato puree and cook for 1 minute
Toast spices in the mixture for 1 minute. Add water if needed to avoid burning
Add rinsed chickpeas
Add all liquid ingredients and bring to a gentle simmer.
Cook for 15-20 minutes until desired consistency
Add juice of half a lime and fresh cilantro
Serve:
Serve with long grain rice and naan
